Our Story

Coffee heritage from the land of Gura, Enrekang.

On the mountain slopes of Enrekang, South Sulawesi, grows legendary Arabica beans known worldwide since the early 20th century as Enrekang Arabica. An altitude of 1,500m above sea level, fertile volcanic soil, and a cool climate impart complex flavors of floral notes, ripe berries, and a smooth body.

COGURA was born from a desire to bring this exceptional coffee to coffee enthusiasts across the archipelago. We work directly with local farmers, meticulously managing every process from red cherry selection to roasting so authentic flavor lives in every brew.
